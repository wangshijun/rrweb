# Use Cases

rrweb's core functionality of recording and replaying web sessions enables a wide range of practical applications. By capturing a high-fidelity log of DOM mutations and user interactions, developers, product teams, and support staff can solve complex problems. This section outlines the primary scenarios where rrweb provides significant value, focusing on the problems it solves without detailing the implementation.

<x-cards data-columns="2">
  <x-card data-title="Bug Reproduction and Debugging" data-icon="lucide:bug">
    Capture the exact conditions and interactions that lead to a bug. Developers can replay the session to observe the issue firsthand, inspect the DOM at any point in time, and debug with complete context. This eliminates the "it works on my machine" problem and dramatically reduces the time spent on issue replication.
  </x-card>
  <x-card data-title="User Behavior Analysis" data-icon="lucide:line-chart">
    Gain qualitative insights into how users interact with your application. Session replays allow product managers and UX designers to watch actual user journeys, identify points of friction, discover usability issues, and understand behavior in a way that quantitative analytics alone cannot provide.
  </x-card>
  <x-card data-title="Customer Support and Co-browsing" data-icon="lucide:users">
    Power real-time customer support by viewing a user's session live (with sensitive data masked). Support agents can provide accurate, contextual guidance without guesswork, leading to faster resolutions and a better customer experience. This is often referred to as "co-browsing."
  </x-card>
  <x-card data-title="Creating Product Demos and Training" data-icon="lucide:video">
    Generate high-fidelity, interactive product tours and tutorials from recorded sessions. Instead of creating mock-ups, you can use real application interactions to create realistic and effective onboarding materials or marketing demos.
  </x-card>
  <x-card data-title="Automated Testing" data-icon="lucide:bot">
    Accelerate the creation of end-to-end tests by converting recorded user sessions into automated test scripts. This helps capture complex user flows and edge cases that are difficult to script manually, thereby improving test coverage and reliability.
  </x-card>
  <x-card data-title="Compliance and Auditing" data-icon="lucide:shield-check">
    Maintain a detailed, replayable audit trail of user activity for compliance and security purposes. In regulated industries like finance or healthcare, session replays provide a verifiable record of user actions within the application.
  </x-card>
</x-cards>

---

By leveraging these use cases, you can significantly improve your development workflows and gain a deeper understanding of your users. To begin implementing these scenarios, proceed to the [Getting Started](./getting-started.md) guide.
